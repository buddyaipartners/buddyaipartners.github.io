# BUDDY AI PARTNERS — Portfolio Overview

BUDDY AI PARTNERS is the parent company and client-services business. The portfolio combines AI and workflow products with delivery work for real-estate clients. The client relationships below reflect the current portfolio structure: KNR Integrity is the first client; Costa Fortune is the second client/project identified in the real-estate work.

## Portfolio map

- [Buddy-ai-partners-company](https://github.com/rahuldhal800-a11y/Buddy-ai-partners-company) — Company website, services, portfolio, and digital-office experience.

## Naming and portfolio guidance

- Keep BUDDY AI PARTNERS clearly identified as the parent company; do not describe the company website as the Buddy AI runtime.
- Label KNR Integrity as client one and Costa Fortune as client two/project in portfolio materials, while keeping their code and data boundaries clear.
- Mark agent/model work as prototype or research unless a specific deployment is verified.
- Avoid publishing client-sensitive information, credentials, personal data, unverified property claims, or internal operational details in public repository descriptions or documentation.
