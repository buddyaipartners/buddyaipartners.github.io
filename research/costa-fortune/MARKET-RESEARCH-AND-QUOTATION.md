## 10. Deal strategy — how to run the meeting

### Opening (5 minutes)
Do not present. Ask three questions:
1. "Of the 126 unsold units, how many are 2 and 3 BHK?" *(Answer: all 72 large units.)*
2. "What does a qualified lead cost you today, and who owns follow-up?" *(Expect no clear answer — that is the opening.)*
3. "If I Google this project right now, which price should I believe?" *(Let them discover the five ranges themselves.)*

### Middle (20 minutes)
Walk the **contradiction table** (§6.2) and the **velocity math** (§4). Then the **construction timeline** (§3). Do not soften these. The developer already knows the project is slow; what they don't know is how visible it is.

Then say the positioning line from §9.1, verbatim.

### Close (10 minutes)
Offer **only** the Diagnostic Sprint. Do not present three tiers in the first meeting — it invites price comparison and delays a decision. Say:

> "Before either of us commits to a bigger number, let me spend five days producing the document you'd otherwise pay a consultant ₹1 lakh for. It's ₹25,000, and it comes off whatever we do next."

### Objection handling

| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| "KNR handles our marketing." | "Good — they should keep doing it. We're not taking the mandate. We're giving you the data layer KNR sells from. Right now their page quotes unit sizes that don't exist in your brochure. That hurts them too." |
| "We already have a website." | "You have a brochure online. It has no price, no floor plans, no WhatsApp, and the domain on your printed brochure doesn't load. That's not a website, it's a liability." |
| "Our price is fixed." | "Then let's justify it. The locality averages ₹4,300–5,250. You're at ₹5,700. That's defensible — but only if the buyer can see the amenity programme, the construction progress and the ORERA record in one place. Right now they can't." |
| "Too expensive." | "One booking on a 2,033 sqft unit is about ₹1.15 crore. The Diagnostic Sprint is ₹25,000. If it finds nothing, I'll tell you." |
| "Can you guarantee sales?" | "No. Anyone who does is lying. What I can guarantee is that every lead is captured, qualified, followed up within five minutes, and reported honestly. That's the part that's actually broken." |

### What not to do
- Do not repeat KNR's "Olympic pool," "skating rink," "EV charging" or "12.4–16.8% CAGR" claims. They are unsupported.
- Do not criticise KNR personally. The founder's history there is an asset, not a talking point.
- Do not quote a website price. Quote an infrastructure price.
- Do not promise a timeline you cannot hold as a solo founder. Tier C in particular needs a realistic capacity conversation.
