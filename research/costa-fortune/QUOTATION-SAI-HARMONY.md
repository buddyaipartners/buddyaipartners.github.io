## 1. Understanding of the requirement

Costa Furtune Crestview is a 146-unit (144 flats + 2 shops), B+S+12 gated community at Kantabada with possession scheduled for August 2028. The project is ORERA-registered and BDA-approved, with a full amenity programme and a strong location on the Chandaka–NH-16 corridor.

At present, the project's digital presence does not reflect the quality of the product:

- The developer website is a six-page brochure site with no pricing, no downloadable floor plans and no direct enquiry channel.
- Seven online sources publish **seven different price ranges** for the same project, along with at least four different unit-size sets.
- The domain printed on the official brochure does not currently resolve.
- The campaign link used in social media returns a 404 error.
- There is no single verified record of the project that sales teams, channel partners and buyers can all rely on.

This proposal addresses those gaps in three stages. It is **not** a brokerage or sales mandate, and it does not replace any existing marketing partner. It builds the developer's own data, CRM and lead-handling infrastructure so that every channel — direct, broker, portal and referral — sells from the same verified information.
