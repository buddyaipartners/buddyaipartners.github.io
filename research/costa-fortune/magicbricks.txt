About Costa Furtune Crestview
Sai Costa Furtune Crestview is a Under Construction project by Sai Harmony Pvt. Ltd in Kantbada Bhubaneswar, offering 1,2,3 BHK Flats configuration. Below are the key details of Sai Costa Furtune Crestview.

Strategically located in Kantbada, Bhubaneswar, Sai Costa Furtune Crestview offers property options within the price range of Rs. 36.9 Lac - 82.5 Lac. The project is designed by Sai Harmony Pvt. Ltd, a well-known and trusted developer in India. As of the latest update, the project is in the Under Construction phase. It is developed over an area of 4443 Sq-m. This project encompasses over 146 units.

There are 12 towers planned within the project. August 2024 marked the official launch of the project. The possession of Sai Costa Furtune Crestview starts from August 2028. You can reach out to this project at Kantabad, Chandaka, Bhubaneswar, Dist- Khordha. Property prices in Sai Costa Furtune Crestview have appreciated by around 4.0% in the last quarter, going up from 5,482/sq. ft in September 2026 to nearly 5,701/sq. ft at present.

If financed through a home loan, you can expect an EMI of nearly Rs. 37,464/month over 30 years. Presently, 7 properties are available for purchase through owners and dealers, offering multiple options to explore. You will find ready-to-move societies like TSL Spring City in the surrounding area.
