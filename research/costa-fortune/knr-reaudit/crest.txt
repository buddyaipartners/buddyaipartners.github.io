Technical Construction Specifications & Materials
Engineered in compliance with Indian Standards and Seismic Zone III structural codes. Premium branded fixtures throughout.
🏗️ Structure & Foundation
Seismic Zone III compliant RCC framed structure engineered with high-grade Fe 550D TMT steel and M25/M30 grade concrete.
🧱 Superstructure & Masonry
Precision-engineered AAC lightweight blocks / high-density fly ash bricks with double-coat internal plaster and weather-resistant external sand-faced plaster.
🏢 Flooring & Skirting
800x800mm or 1200x600mm double-charged glazed vitrified tiles in Living, Dining & all Bedrooms; Anti-skid ceramic tiles in Balconies; Polished Granite in staircases and common lobbies.
🚪 Doors & Windows
Main Entrance: Teak wood frame with designer laminated door & biometric lock provision. Windows: Heavy-duty powder-coated UPVC/Aluminium sliding windows with toughened glass and mosquito mesh provisions.
🍳 Kitchen & Utility
Polished heavy black granite platform counter with premium stainless steel sink (Nirali/Franke grade), 2ft ceramic wall tiles, and dedicated chimney & RO purifier points.
🚿 Bathrooms & CP Fittings
Concealed CPVC/UPVC pipelines, premium CP fittings (Jaquar / Kohler or equivalent), wall-hung EWC with concealed cisterns, and 7ft glazed wall dado.
⚡ Electrical & AC Wiring
100% concealed fire-resistant copper wiring (Finolex / Havells / Polycab), designer modular switches (Schneider / Legrand), dedicated AC points in all rooms.
🎨 Painting & Finishes
Interior: Smooth acrylic wall putty with premium washable acrylic emulsion (Asian Paints Royale / Berger). Exterior: Weather-coat anti-fungal exterior paint.
🛗 Vertical Transport & Backup
Automatic high-speed passenger elevators with Automatic Rescue Device (ARD) from Otis / Schindler / Kone; 100% DG diesel generator power backup.
